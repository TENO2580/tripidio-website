Case Studies

Real results for real businesses

These are the kinds of outcomes our analytics work delivers across industries.

Retail Chain
How a multi-branch retail chain eliminated weekly reporting chaos
A retail chain with 12 branches was spending 16+ hours per week manually compiling sales reports in Excel. We built an automated Power BI dashboard pulling live data — branch performance, product mix, and target tracking — all updated daily without manual input.
80%
Reduction in reporting time. Reports that took 16 hours now take under 2 hours automatically.
Electronics Distribution
Identifying underperforming branches before they became problems
A mobile distribution company had no visibility into which branches were underperforming. We built a branch performance dashboard with target vs achievement tracking, regional heatmaps, and month-on-month comparison — enabling the management team to intervene proactively.
3x
Faster management intervention on underperforming branches due to real-time visibility.
EdTech Company
Sales forecasting that transformed inventory and staffing decisions
An EdTech company was running blind on lead pipeline planning. We built a forecasting model using historical enrollment data, seasonal trends, and run-rate analysis — giving the sales head a 6-week forward view to plan team sizing and marketing spend.
35%
Improvement in lead-to-enrollment conversion by aligning team size to predicted pipeline volume.
Service Center
Excel automation that saved 8+ hours of manual work every week
A service center was manually calculating technician incentives, complaint turnaround times, and daily MIS reports in Excel. We automated the entire process with Excel macros and structured data flows — eliminating errors and freeing up the operations team.
8 hrs
Saved per week on manual report preparation. Zero calculation errors since automation.

FAQ

Common questions

What industries do you work with? +
We work primarily with retail businesses, electronics and mobile distribution companies, service centers, EdTech companies, SMEs, and sales-driven organizations. If your business generates data and needs clearer insights from it, we can help regardless of industry.
How long does dashboard development take? +
A standard Power BI dashboard typically takes 1–2 weeks from data access to delivery, depending on complexity and how clean your existing data is. We deliver a working version quickly and refine based on your feedback. Complex multi-dashboard projects with data engineering may take 3–6 weeks.
Can you automate our Excel reports? +
Yes — this is one of our most requested services. We can automate Excel-based MIS reports, incentive calculations, daily/weekly/monthly consolidations, and multi-source data merging. Most automation projects pay for themselves within the first month in time saved.
Do you work with our existing company data? +
Absolutely. We work with whatever data you have — whether it's in Excel sheets, SQL databases, CRM systems, ERP exports, or a mix of all of them. Our first step is always to understand your current data structure and build around it, rather than asking you to change systems.
Can small businesses use your services? +
Yes — small and medium businesses are our core focus. We specifically built our Starter package for businesses that are too small for large consulting firms but serious enough about growth to invest in proper analytics. You don't need an enterprise budget to get enterprise-quality insights.
Do you provide support after project delivery? +
Yes. All packages include post-delivery support. Starter includes 1 month, Growth includes 3 months, and Enterprise clients get ongoing support as part of the retainer. We also offer separate monthly maintenance plans for clients who want continued dashboard updates and data management.
